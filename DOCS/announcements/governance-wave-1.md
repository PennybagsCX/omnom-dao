## Telegram — full post

🗳️ **Governance Wave 1: the DAO votes on how it votes.**

Quadratic voting is live. Now the community settles the rulebook itself — three governance parameters, one vote each, **voting opens September 22**:

1️⃣ **Global quorum** — 5%, today's 10% default, or the PRD's 20%? How much turnout makes a result legitimate.

2️⃣ **Pass threshold** — simple majority for everything, 60% supermajority for everything, or today's split (supermajority only for chain, tokenomics and technical)?

3️⃣ **Per-type quorums** — keep the seeded 10–15% defaults, or adopt the PRD schedule (up to 25% for chain & tokenomics, 5% for general discussion)?

**Full transparency on the fine print:** these convention votes run at the platform's minimum — a **5% quorum** — because the 10% default they aim to replace was never itself voted on; it shipped as a placeholder. A rulebook vote no turnout could ever legitimize would settle nothing. If any of these votes fails to reach quorum, nothing changes: the current rules stay in force. Simple majority decides, 7-day window each.

Every proposal lays out the decision, the current v1 baseline, every option with its trade-offs, and exactly what changes if it passes. Debate happens right in the comments.

📅 Voting opens **Sept 22** → closes **Sept 29**.
